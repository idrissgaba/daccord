# Reactions and Emoji

You can react to any message with emoji to express yourself without sending a full reply.

## Adding a Reaction

1. Hover over a message and click the **React** button in the floating action bar.
2. The emoji picker opens showing 160 emoji organized into 8 categories.
3. Use the search field to find a specific emoji.
4. Click an emoji to add it as a reaction.

You can also right-click a message and select **React** from the context menu.

## Removing a Reaction

Click your own reaction on a message to remove it.

## Reaction Display

Reactions appear as small pills below the message content, showing the emoji and a count of how many people reacted with it. Your own reactions are highlighted.

## Using Emoji in Messages

To insert an emoji into your message text, open the emoji picker from the composer area.

## Skin Tone

You can set your preferred emoji skin tone in **App Settings > Appearance**.

## Stickers

Click the **sticker** button next to the emoji button in a space channel to
send a sticker. The picker shows the stickers of every space you belong to,
the current one first — you can send another space's stickers too.

Members who can manage emoji upload stickers in **Space Settings → Stickers**
(PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 60 per space), and rename or delete them there. A
deleted sticker shows its name in old messages.

## Super Reactions

A super reaction is a showier reaction: it glows, carries a sparkle and pops
whenever someone adds one. Everyone can use them, as often as they like.

- Right-click (or long-press) a message → **Super reaction**, then pick an emoji.
- Click a super reaction to add or remove yours.
- Super reactions are counted apart from normal reactions with the same emoji.
