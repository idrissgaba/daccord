# Polls

A poll is a message with a question and up to 10 answers that people vote on.

## Creating a Poll

1. In a space channel, click the **poll** button at the left of the message box.
2. Type the question and at least one answer. **Add answer** adds more (up to 10).
3. Pick how long the poll stays open: 1, 4 or 8 hours, 1 or 3 days, or 1 or 2 weeks.
4. Turn on **Allow multiple answers** if people may pick more than one.
5. Click **Post**.

Polls aren't available in direct messages.

## Voting

Click an answer to vote for it. Click it again to take your vote back. On a
single-answer poll, picking another answer moves your vote.

Results (votes and percentages) show once you have voted or the poll has
ended. The line under the poll shows the vote count and the time left.

## Ending a Poll Early

The author, or anyone who can manage messages, can right-click the poll and
choose **End poll**. Nobody can vote on an ended poll; its results stay visible.
